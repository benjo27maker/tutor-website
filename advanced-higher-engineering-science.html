FAQs

Got a question?

What topics do students struggle with the most at Advanced Higher?

At Advanced Higher level, students often struggle most with Control Systems and Feedback, Op-amp Circuits and Structural Analysis. These topics are more mathematical and ask students to apply ideas to unfamiliar systems. As a result, students who understand the concepts but are less confident with the calculations often find them particularly challenging.

What's the most effective way to improve in Advanced Higher Engineering Science?

The quickest way to improve in Advanced Higher Engineering Science is to identify and address your weaknesses. Engineering Science rewards a solid grasp of how systems work, but marks also depend on careful calculations, clear diagrams and applying ideas to unfamiliar contexts. Our tutors are experts at spotting where students lose marks by observing how they approach practice questions, the steps they struggle with and where they tend to get stuck. This allows us to target the areas that need the most attention and help students build confidence and make progress.

How are the lessons structured?

Our online lessons are delivered via Microsoft Teams. All our tutors use iPad screen-sharing technology, allowing students to watch them work through problems live while explaining the reasoning behind each step. Every lesson is tailored to the student's individual learning needs, whether that involves topic revision, live worked examples or past paper practice.
